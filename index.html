What is real here, what is not
  • Density: PREM (Dziewonski & Anderson 1981). Model mass 5.973e24 kg, g at surface 9.82 m/s².
  • Gravity inside Earth: g(r) = G·M(<r)/r², from that density model, integrated numerically.
  • Motion: RK4 integration. With the uniform-density option it matches the textbook formula π√(R³/GM) = 42.2 min.
  • Game rule, not physics: arriving under 20 m/s (or turning around within 100 m of the end) counts as grabbing a ledge. The model itself only outputs arrival speed and time.
  • Rotation option adds centrifugal and Coriolis terms. Tunnel wall is assumed frictionless and perfectly strong.
  • Drag: simple ½ρ·CdA·v² with constant density scaled by shaft pressure. No shock waves, no heating.
  • Not modelled: rock heat (thousands of °C), pressure on the tunnel, melting. No such tunnel can exist.
  • Earth surface image: NASA Blue Marble, via three.js examples.
