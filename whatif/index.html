Fixed limitations: flat terrain, free-field 1/r² spreading, no atmospheric absorption/wind/temperature gradient, uniform clap strength, independent incoherent sources, stationary listener (clap-time position par field computed). Continuum approximation ko early times par 20 ms clamp kiya gaya hai kyunki nearest ~1 m mein discrete clappers dominate karte hain.
